Help shape the next night

Sharing playtest data is optional and off until you choose it. You can play and save local scores without sharing.

What sharing sends

Gameplay totals and choices: runs and stages, sparks, bumps, near misses, combos, bass time and Bass Head use, candy effects, perks and themes. We also record a few menu actions, control type, broad screen-size category and game version.

A random identifier lets us group playtest activity from this browser. This is pseudonymous data, not anonymous data. We do not collect names, email addresses, exact pointer movements, recordings or screenshots through this feature. Arcade initials are not attached to these reports.

Storage and controls

The planned hosting service is Supabase. Raw playtest records are private and deleted after at most 30 days by daily cleanup. Grouped counts without browser or run identifiers may be kept longer. Brief network-derived hashes help limit spam and are deleted within roughly 24–25 hours. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and request metadata in their service logs.

Stop sharing in Sound & settings at any time. This deletes unsent events and stops collection immediately; requests already received cannot be recalled by that switch. Starting sharing during a run applies to your next run. Turning sharing off removes the random identifier from this browser.

Public collection is not enabled until hosting and its log-retention settings have been reviewed. This notice will be updated with those details before activation.

Questions or feedback

Open a GitHub issue. Issues are public: please do not include passwords, private information or identifying playtest data.
