# Forest Freakout assets

`themes/forest-freakout/*.webp`: original AI-generated title, woodland backdrop, Disco Boots, Pool Noodle, Moth Charm and Friendship Bracelet artwork created for this project on 2026-10-16 using the approved Forest Freakout concept. Optimized locally to WebP (title/backdrop at most 1200px; perk art 480px).

`themes/previews/*.webp`: reduced concept-board previews of Moonlit Mycelium and Neon Field Trip. These are not implemented themes.

Fonts are bundled locally from the official google/fonts repository: Lilita One (`ofl/lilitaone/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf`) and DM Sans (`ofl/dmsans/DMSans[opsz,wght].ttf`). Their SIL Open Font License files are retained alongside each font in `fonts/`. No runtime font service is used.

## Moonlit Mycelium

`themes/moonlit-mycelium/*.webp`: original artwork generated with built-in imagegen for the approved Moonlit concept, 2026-10-16. Title/backdrop optimized to at most 1200px; individual perk art to 480px. UI uses a system Georgia serif stack with the existing local DM Sans body font; HUD numbers stay sans-serif for readability.

Prompt direction: luminous woodland storybook, midnight navy, silver sage foliage, lavender mushrooms, pale golden moonlight. Title: square poster, cloaked wanderer facing a glowing tent village, exact “SHAMBHALA SIDE QUEST” title in enchanted serif, no UI or additional text. Backdrop: 3:2 woodland frame with quiet dark center, crescent moon, mushrooms and fireflies at edges, no text. Perks: four separate square illustrations of celestial platform boots, curved foam pool noodle, luna moth/crescent charm, and woven friendship bracelet, no text/UI/borders.

## Neon Field Trip

`themes/neon-field-trip/*.webp`: original artwork generated with built-in imagegen for the approved Neon concept. Title/backdrop optimized to at most1200px; individual perks to480px. Uses the existing locally bundled Lilita One and DM Sans fonts.

Prompt direction: playful psychedelic screenprint/sticker art, deep purple forest, acid lime, hot pink, cobalt and orange, bold ink outlines. Title: square poster with happy moth, smiling mushrooms and festival dancers, exact “SHAMBHALA SIDE QUEST” lettering, no UI or extra text. Backdrop: landscape forest edge frame with quiet purple center, angular pines and neon mushrooms/moths/stars, no text. Perks: four separate square illustrations of rainbow platform boots, smiling pink hollow foam pool noodle, happy multicolor moth, and colorful beaded friendship bracelet; cream sticker outlines and no text or UI.
