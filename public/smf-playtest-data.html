SMF playtest data

Playtest sharing is optional and off until you choose it. You can play and save scores on this device without sharing.

What sharing sends

With your consent, the game sends aggregate results: mode, game version, run outcome, stages cleared and total score; stage, pickup, close-pass and Bass Head use counts; and perk choices. Quick Rush results identify the selected venue. Stage and run results also include your selected movement style: tap, swipe or thumbstick. This is a snapshot of your setting when the result is recorded, not a history of switches or time spent using each style. A random guest identifier groups events during the current session. We do not collect names, email addresses, exact pointer or keyboard activity, recordings or screenshots through playtest sharing.

Online score posting is a separate optional action. If you post, the public board displays your chosen initials, score, stages cleared, mode, Quick Rush venue, input type and received time. Local scores remain on your device unless you choose to post.

Storage and controls

Private playtest records are deleted after 30 days by scheduled cleanup. A short-lived network-derived hash helps limit spam; hosting providers may process IP addresses and request metadata in their service logs.

You can turn sharing off in settings. This stops collection, clears unsent events and aborts pending requests. Events already received cannot be recalled by that switch. A new random guest identifier is created if sharing is enabled again.

Questions or feedback

Open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so please do not include private information.
