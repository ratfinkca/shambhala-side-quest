# SMF interface direction — 2027 campaign references

Date inspected: 2026-11-06. Status: research/design direction, not implemented UI. User decision: use **2026 festival structures** as the venue baseline, and draw **2027 website art inspiration for title GUI and menus**. Stage designs do not inherit the annual festival theme.

## References inspected

- [Official main site](https://shambhalamusicfestival.com/): browser-inspected 2027 landing-page artwork. Black starfield, small white stars/coloured flecks, large irregular white festival lettering, moths, UFO/car imagery and surreal collage elements; rounded cream actions with bright pink accents. This is a visual observation, not a claim about an officially named theme.
- [Official ticket site](https://tickets.shambhalamusicfestival.com/products/tickets): browser-inspected 2027 presentation. Related starfield, magenta brand treatment and large panels, lime circular navigation icons/connector lines, rounded cream controls and an irregular display heading. Text-only retrieval encountered a queue redirect; the normal browser reached the public ticket page. No purchase, checkout, account action or form submission was performed.

These pages may change. The reference is the appearance observed on the date above, not a promise that later campaign assets will remain identical. Existing photographs and artwork have not been downloaded or added as runtime assets by this research pass.

## Proposed game translation

- **Title:** an illustrated cosmic festival poster with an original Side Quest title treatment, a quiet starfield behind it, sparse moth/star/collage accents and a clear primary Play action. Use a few intentional cutout elements rather than filling every empty area.
- **Menus:** dark panels, rounded cream primary buttons, lime progress/selection marks and magenta highlights. A small route/constellation motif can connect menu sections or main-stage progress. Maintain a readable body font, visible focus and large touch targets; expressive display lettering belongs in short headings.
- **Perk/wardrobe cards:** playful festival-sticker framing, restrained cosmic ornament and an obvious earned/locked state. Existing user theme choices need a compatibility pass before replacing title art; do not silently turn a selected style into a different theme.
- **Scores/share cards:** use the same heading/frame language around initials, main clears, score and control label. Decorative campaign art stays outside the essential numbers and actions.
- **HUD:** borrow type/colour sparingly; reserve the field for legible pickups, dancers and bass feedback. No flying collage or bright starfield behind active gameplay. Reduced-motion alternatives remain static.
- **Secrets:** before discovery, retain anonymous covered tiles. No secret name, identifying art, accessibility label or entrance notification leaks through the campaign treatment.

Working palette for later mockups: near-black background, warm cream text/actions, acid-lime selection and fuchsia accent. These are proposed game roles, not extracted production design tokens or a final contrast audit. The websites' custom lettering is a visual reference; choose/approve actual game font/art assets during the identity pass.
