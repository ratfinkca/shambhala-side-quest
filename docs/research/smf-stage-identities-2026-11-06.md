## Evidence and interpretation

Official stage-specific announcements establish advertised bookings and curator intent, not proof that every scheduled performance happened. Artist-uploaded recordings establish the uploader's stated venue/year, not a complete roster. Playlist inclusion alone is not booking evidence. The suggestions below are **our original game interpretation**; tempo/key/palette choices are not measurements of festival recordings.

Research covers 2023–2026, with gaps shown below. Six current official stage photo carousels were visually inspected in a browser; individual capture years are not consistently provided, and some Living Room alt text explicitly refers to 2024. Treat these as silhouette/material references, not six verified 2026 site plans. The dated [2026 photo-album index](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/2026-photo-album-blog) is a further art-reference source, not a claim that every linked album was reviewed. Default visual target proposed: 2026 structures, subject to owner confirmation before art freeze. No full recorded set was audibly auditioned here; linked recordings are listening references for the composition pass.

## Six main-stage briefs

### The Living Room — welcome beside the river

- **Evidence:** the [2024 directors' guide](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/living-room-stage-directors-guide-to-2024) describes an intentional place to slow down around cedars and the river. Its day/night range and broad programming argue against treating it as exclusively gentle house. Confidence: high for setting/breadth, medium for our chosen musical centre.
- **Game visual:** warm sunset water reflections, timber shelter, hanging household lamps and cedar silhouettes; soft amber/teal moving washes. River and seating are perimeter scenery, not new drowning or collision mechanics. Current official photos are shape references with mixed/unknown capture dates.
- **Original music proposal:** 112 BPM warm disco/deep-house pulse; rounded bass, lightly swung hats, soft electric-key chords and a short answering motif. Calm arrival becomes a more grounded night groove. No sampled vocals or borrowed hooks.
- **Encounter:** broad safe central crossing, few speakers at the sides, generous arrival space. Teach click/tap destination movement and collecting before narrower encounters. First secret invitation belongs to this parent, but its identity stays covered until earned.
- **Listening reference:** [CHIMP IT's self-published Living Room 2024 set](https://m.soundcloud.com/chimpitbeats/shambhala-2024-the-living-room?in=chimpitbeats%2Fsets%2Flive-sets), tagged Garage/UKG/140, is a useful counterexample to “only chill music.” Not a measured tempo reference for the proposed 112 BPM composition.

### Fractal Forest — playful funk among the trees

- **Evidence:** the repeated multi-year resident/programming pattern above supports funk, breaks, house and hip-hop as overlapping centres, with substantial bass/DnB variety. Confidence: high for funk/party identity; no single tempo is historical fact.
- **Game visual:** irregular cedar trunks around a central booth, radial canopy details, playful geometric ornaments, saturated greens/pinks and rotating broad light fans. Use original ornament designs rather than reproducing recognizable film-character props visible in older photos.
- **Original music proposal:** 120 BPM syncopated funk breaks, elastic synthesized bass, short brass-like responses and clipped chord hits; trade the main hook and a related response every four bars. Later arrangement can lean house without changing scored timing.
- **Encounter:** curved weaving routes with clear sightlines; authored decorations at the boundary, not invisible tree hitboxes. Smooth passes feel natural among staggered dancer groups. Difficulty rises through routing, not random obstacle clutter.
- **Listening reference:** [Stickybuds' artist profile](https://m.soundcloud.com/stickybuds) lists Fractal sets for 2023, 2024 and 2025; [Burchill's own Fractal 2025 set](https://soundcloud.com/burchillmusic/burchill-shambhala-2025) explicitly identifies the venue. Audition for phrase development and room energy, not reusable musical material.

### The Grove — deep forest and rhythmic curiosity

- **Evidence:** 2023's curator description spans dub/grime/garage/house; 2024's guide spans jungle, house and melodic closing sets; 2025–2026 add further electronic/live breadth. Confidence: high for eclectic forest identity. “Ambient mushroom stage” alone would miss much of the programming.
- **Game visual:** cedar canopy, warm cut-timber structure, garden edges and softly lit path openings; teal/moss with amber accents. Layer distant foliage and slow volumetric-looking sweeps without hiding active objects. This is distinct from Fractal's playful high-contrast geometry.
- **Original music proposal:** 132 BPM spacious two-step/garage with dub chords, round sub notes, ghost percussion and a melodic atmospheric response. Keep actual dancefloor swing rather than a constant ambient pad. A later dub/halftime texture can share the same transport.
- **Encounter:** offset speaker pockets, a few organic ground patches, two broad routes around the centre; the secret clue sits on a safe side route rather than inside maximum bass pressure.
- **Listening reference:** [Mews' Grove 2023 recording](https://soundcloud.com/soundofmewsic/shambhala-2023-the-grove) identifies the stage and supplies a tracklist. The [2024 curator guide](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/the-grove-stage-directors-guide-to-2024) supplies context for very different parts of the same weekend.

### The AMP — the pit and sub pressure

- **Evidence:** 2024's experimental/UK bookings, 2025's broad roster and 2026's explicit dubstep/UKG/halftime programme support a bass-led centre with variety. Confidence: high for musical breadth; 2023 stage-specific roster remains a gap.
- **Game visual:** open pit, prominent speaker stacks, timber/LED stage face and long outward beam fans. The currently served official photograph shows a timber structure; do not blindly reuse the older tent silhouette. Exact 2026 facade needs owner/date confirmation.
- **Original music proposal:** 140 BPM deep dubstep/halftime: kick/sub space, snare on beat three, restrained off-beat percussion, short bass calls and deliberate silence. Use eight-bar question/answer development rather than continuous noisy wobble.
- **Encounter:** heavier-looking speaker islands and clear bass boundaries, with a roomy bypass on both sides. Preserve the existing progressive pressure/Bass Head mechanics; spectacle must not make the zone edge harder to read.
- **Listening reference:** [Just John's self-published AMP 2024 mix](https://soundcloud.com/itsjustjohn/just-john-shambhala-2024) explicitly identifies the stage. The proposed tempo/form comes from our composition direction and 2026 programming context, not a BPM analysis of that recording.

### The Pagoda — architectural light as the headliner

- **Evidence:** 2023–2026 announcements repeatedly combine house/techno and bass; the 2025 director interview explains its long-term evolution and 2017 rebuild. Confidence: high for mixed club/bass identity and recognizable architecture.
- **Game visual:** a tall, tiered central facade, legible roof edges and broad projection-like pattern changes. Pearl/indigo/amber is a working palette; keep bright mapped imagery on scenery, with dimmer playfield washes. Shape and movement carry identity even under reduced motion.
- **Original music proposal:** 128 BPM driving house/techno with a clear kick, evolving arpeggio, controlled bass pulse and a memorable tonal return. A related bass-texture section adds contrast. Larger section changes alter projection motifs, not the hitboxes.
- **Encounter:** balanced twin lanes and offset side stacks; a strong central sightline toward the facade. An authored Pagoda-side invitation leads to the third secret discovery. Stage silhouette is a game interpretation, not a scaled replica.
- **Listening reference:** official [2025 day-by-day director programme](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/2025-pagoda-stage-lineup) and [festival-curated 2023–2026 playlists/live-set index](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/the-ultimate-shambhala-playlist-collection); artist-specific set audition still to do.

### The Village — bass fortress and dawn payoff

- **Evidence:** all four years consistently advertise bass and DnB, with recurring artists across those strands. The [current official stage page](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/stages) describes bass/dubstep/DnB and double-decker viewing platforms. Confidence: high for sound; final year-specific structure remains an art gate.
- **Game visual:** heavy timber towers, round portal/light fixtures and layered viewing decks; charcoal/amber with warm dawn sky beyond the trees. Side platforms are scenery, not walkable elevated lanes. Avoid blindly copying older wraparound catwalk layouts.
- **Original music proposal:** 172 BPM rolling DnB with clear two-step drums, deliberate ghost notes, sustained/reese-style bass and a melodic dawn release. A halftime breakdown creates space; it is not a sudden tempo doubling to increase movement difficulty.
- **Encounter:** the fullest first-tour crowd and bass islands, but broad escape lanes and a safe spawn. Tempo changes audio/lighting cadence only; movement speed is independently capped by the rules.
- **Listening reference:** [official 2024 programme](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/2024-village-stage-lineup) and the official live-set archive above. No fan-uploaded video is treated as artist/owner testimony or as proof of exact musical structure.

## Three secret-stage briefs — design notes, not player-facing spoilers

All names, identifying art and descriptions below remain developer/owner material until the corresponding discovery is earned in the game. Current player tiles stay covered. Parent mappings are the user's explicit scope.

### Rabbit Hole — Living Room discovery

- **Evidence:** [Melo.Nade's artist-uploaded Thursday 2025 recording](https://soundcloud.com/melo-nade/rabbit-hole-thurs-1am-shambhala-2025) has a Rabbit Hole URL and describes a close, energetic room on a Nocturne HSD rig. No complete official multi-year programme was located. Confidence: medium for one performance/room character, low for a universal genre or definitive architecture.
- **Original direction:** an intimate hidden room with warm lamps, mismatched cozy details, a low framed entrance and a compact booth. Playful off-kilter bass/garage at a proposed 140 BPM, with conversational motifs and less visual spectacle than AMP. These are design proposals; lamps/entrance treatment require owner confirmation, not an asserted 2026 plan.
- **Play/reward:** one safe compact circuit; no unavoidable pressure trap. First completed visit earns a small rabbit-themed passport/souvenir idea after discovery. A sealed generic invitation conceals the destination until its parent clear commits the unlock.
- **Gap:** owner or crew confirmation of defining appearance and preferred sound; no inference that a whole venue follows the one linked set.

### Secret Garden — Grove discovery

- **Evidence:** [the Grove director's 2024 guide](https://www.shambhalamusicfestival.com/blog/the-grove-stage-directors-guide-to-2024) explicitly describes workshops by day and a more intimate, varied international night programme. It establishes the parent relationship without a fan map. Confidence: high for 2024 role/relationship, lower for an exact 2026 structure or music roster.
- **Original direction:** a sheltered garden pocket, softly illuminated leaves and a small warm clearing. Proposed 96 BPM dub/downtempo arrangement: open bass, gently swung organic percussion and slow harmonic breathing. This selects one quieter interpretation of a varied programme; it is not an official genre restriction.
- **Play/reward:** gentler visual density, wide routes and a short contemplative bonus visit. A seed/flower souvenir is an original reward proposal. Keep the entrance cue discreet and the undiscovered tile anonymous.
- **Gap:** a dated approved photo/reference and a contemporary stage-specific listening reference. No scraped third-party timetable is used to certify bookings.

### Ikigai Mori — Pagoda discovery

- **Evidence:** [Pagoda director Robbie Campbell's 2025 interview](https://edmhousenetwork.com/behind-the-pagoda-shambhalas-mainstage-vision-with-robbie-campbell/) describes a smaller crew-oriented space that also attracts visiting DJs. [LAAV's own link hub](https://linktr.ee/laav.music) links his 2025 Ikigai Mori recording and [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofW_I92G9t8). Confidence: high for that testimony/performance, low for any single all-years genre.
- **Original direction:** close booth, timber framing and a warm lantern/garden-scale space, contrasted against the monumental Pagoda. Proposed 124 BPM percussive house with an intimate bass/chord conversation. Do not infer traditional Japanese musical instrumentation from the name. Architectural motifs require dated owner reference before copying.
- **Play/reward:** compact looping dancefloor with generous clearance; a small lantern-themed souvenir proposal. First invitation and locked tile conceal the name; successful parent qualification earns the reveal.
- **Gap:** owner-confirmed visual cues and preferred musical centre, plus 2026 reference. The video was located, not audibly/visually audited in full.

## Cross-stage art/music acceptance

- Each stage must be identifiable by silhouette/materials, palette, musical rhythm and spatial encounter, even with menu theme changes or reduced motion. Do not recolour one room nine times.
- Compose original rhythm, harmony and motifs. Reference sets explain the room's range; they are not a source of samples, melodies, voice clips or artist branding. Audition original compositions on headphones and phone speakers before acceptance.
- Keep a coherent four/eight-bar vocabulary and larger 16/32-bar arcs. Fractal swings differently from Grove; AMP's space differs from Village's roll. A faster music grid never increases avatar speed automatically.
- Foreground sparks, dancers, taps and bass edges stay legible. Use broad smooth sweeps, controlled projection changes and static reduced-motion alternatives; no harsh strobing.
- Freeze final structures only after confirming the target year, dated images and the secret-stage gaps with the owner. This pass establishes a build direction, not nine final art approvals.
